MY JOURNEY

My career began from the world of communications, public relations, marketing and commercial execution. Working closely with brands and organizations exposed me to a recurring problem: businesses often invest heavily in campaigns and growth initiatives, but struggle to maintain visibility between strategy and actual execution.

Reports arrive late. Field teams operate with limited visibility. Data is fragmented. Decision-makers do not always know what is happening on the ground. And, ultimately, it becomes difficult to connect activity to actual business results.

That experience changed how I viewed marketing and business growth.

I began asking a different question:

What if the systems used to execute growth could be as intelligent, measurable and accountable as the strategies behind them?

That question became the foundation for my transition from simply delivering marketing services to building products and systems that solve operational and commercial problems.

Through MJC Africa, I have led the development of two technology products—MJC ActivePulse and Mclick—each created from real problems observed through business and community engagements.

MJC ACTIVEPULSE
Turning campaign execution into measurable business intelligence.

The Challenge

Marketing campaigns can involve large teams, multiple locations, budgets, assets, field activities, leads, sales and suppliers. Yet many organizations still manage this complexity through spreadsheets, WhatsApp updates, manual reports and disconnected systems.

This creates a gap between what was planned, what happened and what the business actually achieved.

The Solution

MJC ActivePulse is a campaign management and intelligence platform designed to bring the entire campaign lifecycle into one connected environment.

It enables organizations to manage and monitor:

Campaign planning and budgets
Team deployment and attendance
Field execution and geo-verification
Leads and sales
Audits and compliance
Assets and merchandising
Expenses and payroll reconciliation
Reporting and analytics
Campaign performance and ROI

The product is built around one core idea:

Give decision-makers visibility while the campaign is happening—not after it is over.

I have worked across product strategy, UI/UX, product testing, workflow design, technology development, client discovery and commercialization.

The result is not simply a software product, but a system designed to connect marketing activity to accountability, performance and business outcomes.

MCLICK
Turning grassroots networks into structured growth and income opportunities.

The Challenge

Many businesses want to reach customers beyond traditional channels, but grassroots communities, referral networks and field agents are often difficult to organize, monitor and reward effectively.

At the same time, millions of people participate in community networks without having structured mechanisms through which those networks can generate sustainable economic opportunities.

The Solution

Mclick is a field-force and referral technology platform designed to connect businesses, field teams, communities and customers through structured workflows.

The platform supports:

Field-force management
Customer and member onboarding
KYC and document capture
Referrals and chain selling
Sales workflows
Geo-location
Performance monitoring
Commission and income-generation structures

The product is built around a simple principle:

Technology should not only help businesses reach communities; it should also create value for the people within those communities.

My Role

I have led Mclick from the perspective of product vision, business model, user experience, technology development and commercial application.

This has meant looking beyond the application itself and asking how the technology can be deployed within real ecosystems—from community activations and Chamas to sales networks and grassroots distribution.

A practical example is the work around Chama activation, where technology was used to structure community onboarding, field execution, referrals and sales activity.

This experience reinforced one of my strongest beliefs:

A product becomes powerful when it solves a business problem while creating meaningful value for the people using it.
